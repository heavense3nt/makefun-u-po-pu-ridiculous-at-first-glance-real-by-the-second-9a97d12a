A one-sentence startup pitch that sounds ridiculous at first glance, then real by the second — a dating app that matches people by the contents of their fridge.

The program writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A dating app that matches people by the contents of their fridge, because shared condiments predict compatibility better than star signs — and grocery brands pay for the introduction.
